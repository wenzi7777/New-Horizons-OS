# v2.4.0

**IMU and magnetometer sample rates now follow the scan rate. The magnetometer gains an on/off switch (off by default) and hard/soft-iron calibration, with a reminder when it is time to recalibrate.**

## Rates follow the scan

- The IMU and magnetometer are read once per scan interval (`scan.target_fps`), and the chips' output data rates (ODR) are set to match instead of being fixed.
- BMI270 and BMM350: the ODR is the smallest of 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 Hz that is not below the fps. BMM350 averaging is 8 / 8 / 4 / 2 by ODR.
- BMM150: the ODR is picked from 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 Hz, up to 30 Hz. Above that the same reading repeats.
- Previously the BMI270 ran at a fixed 100 Hz, the BMM350 at 25 Hz, and the magnetometer was read every 50 ms, so magnetometer samples were missed at high fps.
- New `status` fields: IMU `odr_hz`; magnetometer `odr_hz`, `averaging`, `poll_interval_us`, `measured_rate_hz`.

## Magnetometer switch

- New command `set_mag`: `enabled` switches the magnetometer on or off, and `cal_interval_days` sets the calibration reminder interval (0–365 days, default 30, 0 = never). Both are saved and survive a reboot.
- **Off by default, including after updating to this version.** When it is off, the chip is not read, frames carry no mag, and the chip is put to sleep.
- Boards without a magnetometer (v1.0.F) answer `magnetometer_not_supported`. On GCU boards with the IMU off, the magnetometer reports `blocked_by: imu`.
- Switching it on in the Desktop / NH Studio / NH Pocket opens the calibration wizard straight away. Leaving the wizard without applying a calibration switches it off again.

## Hard/soft-iron calibration

- New command `set_mag_calibration` stores an `offset` (µT) and a 3×3 `matrix`, applied as `m_cal = M · (m_raw − b)`. `{clear:true}` removes it. The firmware checks value ranges, the determinant and the condition number.
- The calibration is applied to the stream, `sensor_sample` and apps' `mag()`. `sensor_sample` also returns the uncalibrated `mx_raw` / `my_raw` / `mz_raw`.
- The calibration is stored with the settings; a factory reset clears it.

## Suspected drift

- If the 60-second median of the calibrated |m| stays more than 20% away from the field strength at calibration time for 5 minutes, `calibration.suspect` becomes `true` and the apps suggest recalibrating. It clears after 5 normal minutes or a new calibration. It is kept in RAM only, and it never disables the magnetometer or clears the calibration.

## Compatibility

- In `status.magnetometer`, `"calibration":"available"` is renamed to `"compensation":"factory"|"unavailable"`. `calibration` is now an object describing the calibration (`mode`, `offset`, `matrix`, `field_ut`, `residual_pct`, `calibrated_at_ms`, `interval_days`, `suspect`).
- Desktop v1.18.0 / NH Studio v2.15.0 provide the switch, the calibration wizard and the reminders. Older apps keep working without them.
