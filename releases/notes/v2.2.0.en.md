# v2.2.0

**v2.0.F gains conductance output (µS) and two-way auto range. Boards with Rfb fitted on only some rows are no longer taken for saturated, and calibration works in conductance too. Raw ADC mV is unchanged and stays the default on every board.**

## Reading modes (v2.0.F)

- `set_range` now takes three modes: `raw` (ADC mV, the default), `conductance` (µS) and `auto` (conductance with auto range). Conductance G = (Vout − Vref − offset) / (Vref · Rfb): Vref and Rfb cancel, so changing Vref does not change the reading.
- Conductance and auto range need `rfb_measure` first; with no row measured the reply is `range_rfb_required`. Rows without an Rfb read 0.
- The `virtual` mode and the reference Rfb (`ref_rfb_ohm`) are removed; `virtual` answers `range_mode_removed`. **A virtual or auto setting saved by an earlier version is reset to raw mV at Vref 330 mV after the update.**
- A unit change restarts the filter. Leaving auto, or `clear_rfb`, puts Vref back at 330 mV.

## Rfb fitted on some rows only

- `rfb_measure` reports and saves a status per row: `ok`, `no_feedback` (not fitted), `saturated`, `open`, `adc_failed`.
- Rows without an Rfb (the op-amp sits at the rail with no feedback) no longer count as saturated or as the peak, so auto range no longer drives Vref down to 100 mV because of them.

## Two-way auto range

- On saturation Vref steps down by ×0.8, to no lower than 100 mV.
- After 5 s without saturation, and 5 s observed at the current Vref, it steps up as the peak predicts (target peak 2000 mV, at most ×1.25 a step, no step under ×1.03). The ceiling is the lower of the Vref limit and 750 mV; steps are at least 400 ms apart, and frames older than 1.5 s are ignored.
- New status fields: `auto_ceiling_mv`, `window_peak_mv`, `auto_steps`, `saturated_rows`, `saturated_frames`, `rfb_status`, `output_unit`.

## Range information in every frame

- Stream packets (v5) carry a new range extension (type 0x02, 8 bytes): the unit (mV / µS), whether auto is on, the frame's Vref and the saturated-row mask, placed before the app events. Only v2.0.F sends it.
- In conductance the `raw_adc` block carries the ADC reading itself, before compensation, so a recording can be recomputed. `matrix_sample` replies now include `unit`.

## Calibration in conductance

- A calibration profile records its unit (`unit`: mV or uS). Existing profiles count as mV and are not affected.
- When the output unit differs from the profile's, the profile is kept but not applied (`output_mode` reads `raw`, `unit_matches` is false) until the unit matches again; `calibration_enable` then answers `calibration_unit_mismatch`. An empty profile never mismatches.
- A new calibration session runs in the current output unit. If the saved profile is in the other unit the draft starts empty, and saving it replaces that profile. The reading mode cannot change during a session (`set_range` answers `calibration_session_active`); if the unit changes anyway, captures and saving answer `calibration_unit_changed`.
- Stand-alone zeroing answers `calibration_unit_mismatch` while the saved levels are in the other unit.
- In µS the readout curve is identity. A sample read while the ADC was saturated is recorded as saturated, and a level that saw saturation in most of its blocks is left out of the fit. Sensors on rows without an Rfb are marked `no_rfb` (point status `r`, counted in `cells_no_rfb`) and are not failures.
- New status fields: `unit`, `active_unit`, `unit_matches`, `draft_unit`.

## Compatibility

- An older Desktop or NH Studio that does not know the range field may fail to work out the sensor count when it does not know the matrix shape. Update to Desktop v1.14.0 / NH Studio v2.10.0 along with this firmware.
- Other boards behave as before.
