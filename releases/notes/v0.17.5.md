# v0.17.5

## v1.5.F battery indicator

- The second onboard WS2812B now represents charge continuously from red at
  0% through yellow to green at 100%, using the configured onboard brightness
  (30% by default).
- While charging, that same SoC colour breathes without switching fully off.
  At or below the configurable low-battery threshold it flashes quickly when
  not charging; a value of `0` disables the warning.
- `set_indicators` now persists
  `battery_led.low_battery_threshold_percent` (0–25) and reports its v1.5.F
  support in the indicator status. Other hardware tracks retain their existing
  system-light behaviour.

## Battery telemetry contract

- The battery status continues to expose MAX17048 voltage, SoC and rate so
  clients can estimate remaining or charging time without inventing values.
- Existing manual profile, auto-ID detection and conservative charge-current
  safeguards are unchanged.

## Hardware-track compatibility

- v1.0.F and all GCU LTS images are rebuilt at this release version with their
  existing LED and battery behaviour preserved.
