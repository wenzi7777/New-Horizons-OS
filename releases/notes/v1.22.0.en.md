# v1.22.0

**TIA-CTL v2.0.F: Rfb measurement, virtual and auto range, GPIO45 leak compensation and the Vref sweep.**

## Measuring Rfb

- New `rfb_measure`: with every column released, it measures each row's Rfb through the 10 kΩ calibration network (Vout = Vref × (1 + Rfb / 10k)).
- A single-Vref measurement reads about 2.5 % high: the ESP32's ADC is a few mV off at low voltage (about −8 mV on ADC1, +10 mV on ADC2). So it measures six Vref points from 100 mV and takes Rfb from the ratio of the readings' slopes against Vref, which cancels the offset and the gain. A soldered 20k reads 20.01–20.03 kΩ, ADC1 and ADC2 agreeing.
- The result and each row's ADC offset are kept in NVS across reboots. Each row reports a status: `ok`, `no_feedback` (no Rfb soldered), `saturated` (too large for this Vref; measure again with a lower `vref_mv`) or `open`.

## Range

- New `set_range`: `raw` (as read), `virtual` (re-expressed as the reference Rfb would have read it at 330 mV, 20 kΩ by default, so a calibration made on a 20k board still applies) and `auto` (virtual, and lowers Vref while rows hit the rail).
- `auto` only moves Vref below 330 mV, walks back after 20 s of quiet and never goes above it.
- `virtual` and `auto` need Rfb to have been measured.

## GPIO45 leak compensation

- While another column is scanned, column 13's sensors reach ground through R27 (100 kΩ) and add a small current to every row. It is now subtracted from every cell using column 13's reading from the previous frame (it can be switched off in `set_range`).

## Vref sweep and sampling

- New `vref_sweep`: holds the pressure, steps Vref and records the raw matrix at each step, to tell whether a material is a pure resistance (its Rs does not move with Vref). Compensation is paused meanwhile, and Vref and the calibration switch are restored afterwards.
- New `matrix_sample`: returns the median matrix, raw or as the stream carries it.
- `status` gains `range`: the mode, the Rfb and offsets, and how many rows hit the rail in the last frame.

## Compatibility

- Only v2.0.F is affected; other boards are unchanged.
- Pairs with Desktop v1.7.0 (the Vref and range panel).
- The range behaviour under pressure (consistency across Vref, leak compensation, auto range) has not yet been verified with a real sensor matrix.
