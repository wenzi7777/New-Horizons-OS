# v1.19.1

**Hub (ESP-NOW) mode: devices now find a Hub on channel 12 or 13.**

## Fixes

- **The channel scan covers 1–13.** A Hub's channel follows the AP it joins, so a device hops through the channels to find it. The scan list already held 1–13, but the radio starts in ESP-IDF's default country `"01"` (channels 1–11 only). A device in ESP-NOW mode never joins an AP, so it never learns a wider country from a beacon, and the hops to 12 and 13 failed without a trace. The Hub, by contrast, takes its AP's country, so behind an AP on 12 or 13 the Hub connected but devices could never find it.
- ESP-NOW mode now fixes the country to Japan (`JP`: channels 1–13; 14 is 802.11b-only and not used by APs) at startup.
- A refused channel switch is now logged on serial as `[espnow_pairing] esp_wifi_set_channel(N) -> error`, once per channel per boot. Before, it was invisible.

## Compatibility

- ESP-NOW (Hub) mode only. Wi-Fi mode is unchanged and still takes its country from the AP.
- No Hub, Gateway or Desktop update needed; Hub v0.12.1, released alongside, is a separate change.
- With the AP on channels 1–11, nothing changes.
