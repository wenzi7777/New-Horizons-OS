# v1.22.1

**TIA-CTL v2.0.F: Rfb measurement no longer pulled low by Wi-Fi, a 700 mV default Vref ceiling, and range fixes.**

## Rfb measurement

- Rows 10–13 are on ADC2. While Wi-Fi is busy an ADC2 read sometimes fails, and that read used to be averaged in as 0 mV, so the stored Rfb and offset came out low. Failed reads are now skipped. A row with no good read at all is reported as `adc_read_failed` and nothing is stored for it; measure again.
- The measurement's highest Vref is always below 750 mV, whatever the ceiling.

## Vref ceiling

- The default ceiling is now 700 mV instead of 800 mV. A reset that skips the normal shutdown (watchdog, brown-out) leaves the DAC at its voltage, and at about 800 mV the GPIO45 boot strap can be misread until power is cut. 700 mV leaves a margin. A ceiling you already set is kept.

## Range

- `set_range` with `clear_rfb`: clearing Rfb from auto mode puts Vref back at 330 mV at once (it used to stay where auto had lowered it).
- Auto mode: once streaming has stopped for about a second, an old saturation record no longer keeps lowering Vref.
- After `set_vref`, compensation uses the new Vref at once.
- If restoring Vref or the calibration switch fails at the end of an Rfb measurement or a Vref sweep, the reply carries `warnings` instead of a plain ok.

## Compatibility

- v2.0.F only; other boards are unchanged.
- Goes with Desktop v1.8.0 and NH Studio / Pocket v2.4.0 (the new Vref panel).
- Rows 10–13 measured with Wi-Fi on are still to be checked on a real board.
